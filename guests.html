Import Guests by Date

Choose the Guests by Date export from ResNexus (CSV). It is opened here in your browser only. From each row this page keeps the reservation number, the start date and nights, the city and state, and the three-digit area code — nothing else. Importing a range that overlaps an earlier import is safe: a reservation already saved is updated, never doubled.
Privacy: guest names, additional guests, email addresses and phone numbers are never saved, never shown and never sent anywhere. The file is read on this computer; the columns holding them are not even looked at, except that the first three digits of a phone number (the area code) are kept to estimate a region when the city is blank. Before anything is written to the sheet, every value is checked once more and the save is refused if anything resembling an email address or a phone number is found.
