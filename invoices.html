Choose one or more Mendes invoice PDFs. Each one is read here in your browser and checked against its own arithmetic: the line totals must add up, and merchandise, freight, delivery and tax must equal the invoice total. An invoice that does not add up is held back and named below — it is never saved with wrong numbers. Invoices already saved are skipped, so choosing the whole folder again is safe.
Privacy: the PDFs are opened on this computer only. They are never uploaded anywhere. What gets saved to the sheet is the invoice number, dates, the five totals, and each line's codes, description, quantity and prices.
